## The decision

**Lead niche: faceless home & cleaning gadgets.** Highest visual proof (mess →
spotless in seconds), $10–30 impulse price, no face on camera, and the demo *is*
the ad. Best barrier-to-conversion ratio for someone starting from zero.

**Product archetypes to target** (pick specific SKUs from the Affiliate
marketplace once you qualify — score each in the Product Scorer tool first):

| Archetype | Why it works | Typical price | Commission band |
|---|---|---|---|
| Electric spin scrubber | Dramatic grime→clean demo, ASMR-friendly | $20–30 | 10–18% |
| Mini bag / chip sealer | Instant satisfying result, cheap impulse | $10–15 | 10–15% |
| Grout / crevice cleaning gel | Extreme before/after, oddly satisfying | $10–18 | 12–20% |
| Reusable "magic" lint / pet-hair roller | Fast visible pickup, repeat-use | $12–20 | 12–18% |
| Drain snake / clog remover | Shock-value result, high save-rate | $10–16 | 10–15% |

Home/wellness commission runs ~12–15% standard, up to ~22% aggressive; cheap
viral gadgets ($10–30) are the volume drivers that trigger FYP pushes.

## Phase 0 — Setup (Today, 2026-09-02, ~1 hour)

**DO THIS TODAY:**
1. Create a fresh TikTok account. Business or Creator, niche-named (e.g. `@thecleanhack`, `@satisfyingcleanhq`). Not your personal account.
2. Set bio: what the account is ("Cleaning gadgets that actually work 🧼"). No link yet.
3. Follow 15–20 top accounts in cleaning/satisfying/home niches. Study their 3 top videos each — note the hook style and shot length.
4. Film raw footage: order or use ONE cleaning gadget you already own. Shoot 5–10 clips of it working (close-up, good light, steady). You'll need b-roll before you can post.
5. Install a free captions app (CapCut) — burned-in captions are non-negotiable for retention.

## Phase 1 — Grow to affiliate eligibility (Weeks 1–3)

Goal: cross ~1,000 followers so Affiliate unlocks. You post *value/satisfying*
content now; monetize once eligible.

- **Cadence:** 3 posts/day, every day. The algorithm judges each video alone, not your follower count — volume is how you find a winner fast.
- **Format mix per day:** 1 problem→demo, 1 before/after, 1 "3 ways to use it" or trend-audio clip.
- **Rule:** when one video outperforms, immediately make 3–5 variations of it. Ride winners, drop losers.

## Phase 2 — Monetize (Week 4+, once Affiliate is unlocked)

1. Apply: TikTok Shop → Affiliate. Browse the marketplace.
2. Score 10–15 candidate products in the **Product Scorer** artifact. Keep only GOs.
3. Pick 2–3. Request samples or use footage you can legally use.
4. Add product links to your best-performing format. Keep the *format*, swap in the product.
5. Track which posts drove clicks vs sales. Double down on converters.

## 3 ready-to-film scripts (faceless — voiceover + b-roll)

### Script 1 — Problem-agitate demo (electric spin scrubber)
- **HOOK (0–3s, spoken):** "If your shower still looks like this after scrubbing, you're wasting your arm."
- **DEMO (shot by shot):**
  - Shot A: close-up of grimy grout/tile (the "before"), 1.5s
  - Shot B: scrubber powering on, brush spinning, 1s
  - Shot C: single slow pass revealing clean streak — hold on the reveal, 3s
  - Shot D: full clean surface, 2s
- **CTA (spoken):** "Link's in my shop — takes 2 minutes, not 20."
- **CAPTION (on screen):** "POV: you find out scrubbing was optional 🧼"
- **Hashtags:** #cleantok #cleaninghacks #satisfying #homehacks #tiktokshopfinds

### Script 2 — Before/after ($12 vs the hard way) (grout gel)
- **HOOK (0–3s):** "This $12 gel did what a toothbrush and 40 minutes couldn't."
- **DEMO:**
  - Shot A: black/moldy grout line, finger pointing, 2s
  - Shot B: squeezing gel along the line, 2s
  - Shot C: timelapse wipe → white grout, 3s
  - Shot D: side-by-side before/after freeze, 2s
- **CTA:** "Grab it in my shop before it sells out again."
- **CAPTION:** "I gasped. 😮 #beforeandafter"
- **Hashtags:** #cleantok #grout #cleaninghack #oddlysatisfying #homefinds

### Script 3 — "3 ways to use it" listicle (mini bag sealer)
- **HOOK (0–3s):** "3 things you didn't know this $10 gadget could do."

## Reality check (read this)
- 100k views/month in a converting niche ≈ **$200–800** in commissions. A rare
  500k-view hit on a well-matched product can do $2k–8k in a week — rare, not the plan.
- Most videos will flop. That's normal. Volume + variations on winners is the whole game.
- Commissions claw back on refunds — only promote ≥4.5★ products.
